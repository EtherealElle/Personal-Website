# How Cleo writes

Reference for anything drafted on Cleo's behalf — replies to enquiries, review asks,
photo captions. Drawn from the copy already on the site, which is the voice that got
the responses.

## The shape of it

Short sentences. Concrete nouns. The specific thing, not the category.

> Doors that stick, drag, or won't latch, set to swing clean.

> Close enough to show up when I say I will.

> Holes, cracks, water damage, and old plaster patched and finished smooth, ready for paint.

No adjective stacking, no "passion", no "dedicated to excellence", no "team" — it's
one person and the site says so. First person singular. Contractions are fine.

## Rules for a reply

1. **Answer the actual question first.** If they asked whether you do drywall, the
   first line is yes or no, not a greeting paragraph.
2. **Say what happens next and when.** "I can come look Thursday afternoon" beats
   "let me know a good time". One concrete offer, easy to decline.
3. **Never quote a price.** Not a range, not a ballpark, not "probably around". The
   site says free written estimates and that is the promise: look at it, then price it.
   The only numbers that may be repeated are the published starting prices on
   `/small-jobs`, and only as the published starting price.
4. **Never commit to a date.** Propose; don't book. Cleo confirms.
5. **Two or three photos, maximum.** Only if they genuinely resemble the job. A
   carport photo in reply to a drywall patch reads as a form letter.
6. **No claim of being licensed or insured.** Cleo holds an LLC and an occupational
   tax certificate, and neither a residential contractor licence nor general
   liability cover. This never appears in a draft in any form.
7. **Length.** A text is two to four lines. An email is under 120 words.
8. **Sign off** as just `Cleo` — no title, no company block.

## What's true and worth saying

- Free written estimates, and a written agreement before work starts.
- One person doing the work, start to finish — nobody subcontracted in.
- Repairs and small jobs are genuinely welcome, not tolerated.
- Older houses are the interesting ones.
- Queer-owned and woman-owned. Stated plainly where it's relevant, not as a pitch.
- Working across Atlanta and the east side; based an hour south in Jackson.

## What to avoid

| Not this | This |
| --- | --- |
| "Thank you for reaching out to Cleo Valentine Builds!" | "Hi Jess —" |
| "We would be happy to assist you with your project." | "Yes, that's exactly the kind of job I take." |
| "Prices typically range from $300–600." | "I'd want to see it before I price it." |
| "Please don't hesitate to contact me." | "Text me a photo and I'll tell you what I think." |
| "I'm licensed and insured." | (nothing — it isn't true) |
| "Let me know your availability." | "Thursday or Friday afternoon work for me." |
